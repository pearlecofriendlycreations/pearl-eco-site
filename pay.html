Before you pay

Safety and trust

How we confirm a payment

We check our own records — never a screenshot alone.
When you pay, we open our own Mobile Money app, merchant portal or bank statement and match the transaction ID, the amount in UGX and the time against your order. A screenshot on its own is never accepted as proof of payment, because screenshots can be edited. If we cannot find the transaction in our own records, the order is not confirmed and we will ask you to check again with your network or bank.

Our refund rule

  • If we cannot supply your item — a full refund of what you paid, including the delivery fee, within 3 working days of your request.
  • If the item arrives damaged — send a photo within 24 hours of delivery. We replace it free or refund you in full.
  • If you change your mind — returns accepted within 7 days, provided the piece is unused and in its original condition. The return delivery cost is paid by the customer.
  • Custom and made-to-order pieces — these are made for one customer, so they can only be refunded if they are faulty, not because you changed your mind.
  • Refunds are sent back by the same method you paid with, or by Mobile Money if you paid cash.

Delivery

  • Delivery cost is quoted on WhatsApp once we know your location — it is never guessed.
  • Kampala, Wakiso and Entebbe: usually 1–3 working days for ready pieces.
  • Custom orders: we confirm a making time before you pay.
  • Outside these areas: we arrange upcountry transport with a bus or courier, quoted separately.
  • You always see the total — item plus delivery — in UGX before you pay anything.

Why we chose these methods

Our customers are in Uganda, so MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money come first: almost every adult here has a phone that can pay, no bank account is needed, and the money arrives instantly. Cash on delivery matters because many buyers want to inspect a handmade item before parting with money — trust is still built in person. Bank transfer is there for business customers such as schools, hotels and offices who need a receipt for their accounts department. Card payment through a licensed provider covers the few customers paying from outside Uganda. We do not accept cryptocurrency: the Bank of Uganda has said it is not legal tender in Uganda, and taking it would put our customers at risk with no protection.
