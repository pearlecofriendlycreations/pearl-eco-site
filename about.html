Our story

It started with one question

What if the things we throw away could become the things we treasure?

Discarded materials are everywhere. We see them differently. To us, each one is a story waiting for skilled hands — a plastic bottle waiting to become a sunflower, banana fibre waiting to become an eagle, a handful of beads waiting to become something you never want to take off.

Every piece we make carries the bold colour, rhythm and craftsmanship of African design, shaped into a modern, refined style. From hand-beaded jewellery that catches the light to upcycled décor that gives a room its character, our work turns what was left behind into something worth showing off.

What we stand for

Four promises we keep.

Handmade, never machine-made

Every bead is strung and every frame finished by hand. Small variations are proof a real person made it.

Nothing goes to waste

Plastic cuttings, discarded hair, banana fibre and bamboo are rescued from the bin and given a second life.

Honest prices in UGX

Every price on this site is in Uganda shillings. What you see is what you pay.

Made for you

Want a colour, a size or a design of your own? We will make it, and send photos before you pay.

How it is made

From discarded to desired

The same four steps turn a bin bag into something you would hang on your wall.

1

Collect

We gather plastic cuttings, banana fibre, bamboo and loose beads instead of buying new raw material.

2

Prepare

Materials are cleaned, cut, sorted by colour and dried in the sun so they last for years.

3

Craft

Beads are strung, shapes are wired and each panel is arranged by hand until the design sits right.

4

Finish

Art pieces are framed, jewellery is checked clasp by clasp, then photographed for the catalogue.
