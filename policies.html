Policies, in plain language

Delivery, returns, privacy, terms and where every image on this site came from. Nothing hidden.

1. Delivery

  • Where we deliver: Kampala, Wakiso and Entebbe. Other parts of Uganda are arranged case by case using a bus or courier service.
  • Cost: quoted on WhatsApp once we know your location. Delivery cost is never added after you have agreed to pay.
  • Time — ready pieces: usually 1–3 working days within Kampala and surrounding areas.
  • Time — custom pieces: we confirm a making time before you pay, because every custom item is made after you order.
  • Proof of delivery: the rider or our representative collects your signature or a verbal confirmation, which we note against your order.
  • Upcountry: transport is arranged with a licensed bus or courier. You collect from the agreed park or agent, and the fee is quoted up front.
Delivery is only ever agreed on WhatsApp. We will always tell you the item price, the delivery cost and the total in Uganda shillings before you pay.

2. Returns & refunds

  • 7 days to change your mind on ready-made pieces, provided they are unused and in their original condition. Return delivery is paid by the customer.
  • 24 hours to report damage. Send us a photo within 24 hours of receiving a damaged item and we will replace it free of charge or refund you in full.
  • Faulty item: we repair, replace or refund, and we cover the return delivery cost.
  • Custom and made-to-order pieces cannot be returned for a change of mind, because they were made for one customer. They can always be returned if they are faulty.
  • Refund timing: within 3 working days of us agreeing the refund, using the same method you paid with — or Mobile Money if you paid cash.
  • How to start a return: message us on WhatsApp with your order details and a photo.

3. Privacy notice

This notice explains what we do with your personal information, in line with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number, when you message us on WhatsApp.
  • Your delivery address or meeting point, when you place an order.
  • Your email address, only if you give it to us for a receipt or invoice.
  • Payment confirmation details — for example a Mobile Money transaction ID.

This website has no login, no database and no online form. It does not store your personal information. Messages go directly to our WhatsApp account, which is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your questions and prepare a quote.
  • To make, pack and deliver the item you ordered.
  • To confirm payment and issue a receipt.
  • To handle returns, refunds and warranty claims.

Who can see it

  • Only the Pearl Eco-Friendly Creations team handling your order.
  • The delivery rider or courier, and only the address and phone number needed to deliver.
  • Our bank or mobile money provider, for payment processing.
  • We never sell, rent or share your information for marketing, and we never give it to anyone else for their own use.

How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records: 7 years, to meet Uganda's tax and accounting requirements.
  • General WhatsApp enquiries: deleted after 24 months if no order resulted.
  • After that, your information is permanently deleted or securely destroyed.

Your rights

  • You may ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • You may ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • You may ask us to delete your information, unless we must keep it by law.
  • You may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office in Uganda if you believe your rights have been infringed.
  • To do any of the above, email info@pearlecofriendly.co.ug or message us on WhatsApp. We respond within 7 working days.

Keeping it safe

  • Account access is protected with two-step verification and strong, unique passwords.
  • Order records are kept in a private shared folder accessible only to the team.
  • We never place personal phone numbers, passwords or payment details in public files or on this website.
  • If a data breach occurs, we notify affected customers and the relevant Ugandan authority as required by law.

4. Terms of sale

  • All prices are in Uganda shillings (UGX). No other currency is quoted on this website.
  • An order is only confirmed once we message you on WhatsApp to confirm availability and the total. Nothing is reserved before that.
  • Every piece is handmade. Small differences in colour, shape and size from the photographs are normal and are not defects.
  • Prices may change without notice, but the price you are quoted on WhatsApp is the price you pay.
  • Payment must be completed before a custom item goes into production.
  • We are not responsible for delivery delays caused by weather, strikes, traffic or other events outside our control.
  • Our total liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for that order.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of Uganda.
  • Questions about any policy? Message us on WhatsApp — we are happy to explain.
This is a student project for BUC3106 at Makerere University Business School. The website is a course assignment. No real orders are fulfilled through it and no real money changes hands here. Payment details shown on the How to Pay page are marked SAMPLE and are not active.
