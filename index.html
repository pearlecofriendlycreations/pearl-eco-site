Our story

Nothing is waste until we stop seeing its beauty.

Pearl Eco-Friendly Creations began with a simple question: what if the things we throw away could become the things we treasure?

  • Discarded plastic, banana fibre and bamboo, rescued and reimagined.
  • Every piece carries the colour, rhythm and craftsmanship of African design.
  • Made by hand, so no two pieces are ever exactly alike.
  • Style with a conscience — heritage you can see, a planet you help protect.

How to order

Three steps, no account needed

You never type your card details into this site. Everything happens on WhatsApp, where you can see who you are talking to.

1

Pick your piece

Browse the collection and note the name and price. Every price is in UGX.

2

Tap the WhatsApp button

It opens WhatsApp with your message already written. Just press send.

3

Confirm and pay

We confirm availability, agree delivery, then you pay by mobile money or cash.
